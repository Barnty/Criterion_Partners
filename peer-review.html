Why it counts

An invitation is not evidence. A completed review is.

The criterion asks for evidence that you have judged the work of others. A request to review, on its own, shows very little. What carries weight is confirmation from the journal that the review was completed.

That is why we show certificates and not invitation emails. Each one here was issued by the journal after the review was done.
