Straight answers

Questions people ask first.

Is this the kind of service USCIS is scrutinising?

No, and the distinction is the reason we exist. The practices drawing scrutiny are paid journals, traded citations, bought awards and placed press. We refuse all four, in writing, in your engagement letter. Everything we build is either work you actually did or an opportunity you actually earned.

Can you guarantee approval?

No. Anyone who guarantees it is telling you something they cannot know. What we can do is tell you honestly whether your profile is close, and what closing the distance would take.

Do you file the petition?

No. We are not a law firm and nothing we give you is legal advice. We build and organise the evidence, then hand it to the immigration attorney you choose.

What if I have no publications at all?

Then that is where we start. Most people in that position have real work that has never been written up. Turning it into a publishable paper takes three to six months, and it has to be your work.

How long until I am petition-ready?

Six to twelve months for most profiles. Three if you already meet several criteria. If someone offers you three weeks, be careful.

What fields do you work in?

All of them. STEM, economics, medicine, engineering, business, law, policy, social sciences, the arts. The ten criteria are the same everywhere. The evidence that satisfies them is field-specific.

Will you tell me if I am not a good candidate?

Yes, and we do it regularly. Taking money to build a case that will not clear the bar helps nobody.
