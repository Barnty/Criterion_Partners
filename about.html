About

We build records that hold up.

Criterion Partners is a profile building consultancy for EB-1A candidates. We are not a law firm. We do the work that makes a petition strong before an attorney files it.

Founder

Adeyeye Barnabas

Adeyeye Barnabas is the founder of Criterion Partners. He started the firm on a simple conviction: talented people should not have to choose between a weak petition and a dishonest one.

He leads the firm's work with researchers and professionals across 45 countries, helping them turn real work into evidence that satisfies the EB-1A criteria: publications in journals with genuine peer review, reviewer appointments, memberships that are earned, and recognition a stranger can verify.

His rule for every engagement is the same. If an officer spent ten minutes checking a piece of evidence, it should look stronger, not weaker.

Our standards

Four things we will not do.

These are the shortcuts that put petitions at risk. We refuse them in writing, in every engagement letter.

  • Pay-to-publish journalsIf a journal accepts your paper in 48 hours, it will not survive a second look.
  • Traded citationsA citation ring is the easiest pattern to spot from the outside.
  • Awards you can buyIf the entry fee is the only barrier, an officer will find that out.
  • Placed pressA release on a site that publishes anything is not media coverage.
